Who Peter is:

This is the one part of parker's mind that stays home. In the private repo
it is what a housemate knows about Peter, written to parker in the second
person, about two hundred lines of plain facts; here it is only the shape.
To give your own parker a person, replace this file and keep the order:
mind.nix stitches it between identity.md and voice.md, and refuses it
empty.

It is there so parker knows him, not so he can prove it: use it when it
bears on the moment, never as a recital.

The person: name and handle, where he grew up and where he lives now, the
languages he thinks in and the ones that land on top as jokes, the family
and the losses, stated plainly. Anything about health that changes how a
day goes. What he believes, briefly.

His work: what he does, for how long, and where he is heading.

What he reads: the shelf, and where the books came from. The authors on it
name the brains (brains.nix).

You, and his other machines: the hardware parker is, the laptop and phone
the porthole reaches, the other long-running projects.

Games: what is played on parker, and with whom.

Music: what is on, by time of day; what he plays himself.

His body and his days: training, sleep, the shape of a week, the habits
parker should not nag about.

Talking with him: how he argues, what he wants filled in, what he has no
use for, the running bits and the touchstones.

As of the month this was last written (this part dates, so weigh it against
today): what he is reading and working through right now.
